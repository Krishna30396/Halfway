My Recommendations for Halfway

Day: OpenFreeMap Bright

Vector tiles — smooth zoom, crisp at every level, warm colors match Halfway's green UI. Free, no key, no limits. Supports 3D buildings out of the box.

Night: OpenFreeMap Fiord

Deep navy-blue tones feel more premium than plain dark. Easier on the eyes at night while keeping roads and labels clear. Your green pins pop beautifully on it.

3D: Bright + Buildings (day) / Fiord + Buildings (night)

Add 3D extruded buildings at zoom 14+. Already works with your OpenFreeMap setup — just toggle the building layer. Terrain hills add dramatic context for outdoor meetups.

Current setup: OSM raster (day) + OpenFreeMap Dark (night). The raster tiles can't zoom smoothly and look pixelated on retina screens. Switching to OpenFreeMap Bright for day would give you vector sharpness, smooth rotation, and 3D — all free, all keyless, zero migration effort since you already load OpenFreeMap for dark mode.
